# Voice File for WhatsApp — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026.

## Data handled

The extension runs only on WhatsApp Web. It reads the audio file you explicitly choose and the current conversation heading (which may contain a contact name or phone number) to detect conversation changes. Audio and conversation information are handled locally to prepare the voice message and keep the operation in the selected conversation. The extension does not export contacts or chat history.

## Processing and retention

The extension decodes the selected audio in your browser and provides it as the input to a WhatsApp recording request. It does not persist selected audio or conversation headings in extension storage. It has no backend, analytics, advertising, cookies, or developer-operated data collection endpoint. Audio and conversation headings are not sent to the developer, and no developer or employee has access to them.

## WhatsApp

The selected audio enters WhatsApp's recording flow. You review the recording and decide whether to send it. WhatsApp handles recordings and messages under its own terms and privacy policy. The extension never clicks Send automatically. Audio is shared with WhatsApp solely to provide this feature; when you send it, WhatsApp delivers it to the recipient you selected.

## Limited use

Data is used only to prepare your selected audio as a voice message. It is not sold, used for advertising, used for unrelated purposes, or used for creditworthiness or lending. The extension's use and transfer of user information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

Choose only files you want WhatsApp to record. You can cancel using WhatsApp's recording controls. Reloading WhatsApp clears the current extension session. Remove the extension through Chrome's extension settings to uninstall it.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact on the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
